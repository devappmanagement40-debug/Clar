---
name: Demo referrals
description: How to treat fictitious referrals and financial-looking activity examples
---

When a user explicitly requests fictitious referrals saved to their real account, treat them as demonstration records: clearly label them, prevent sign-in and direct contact with invented numbers, and do not grant balances, purchases, commissions, or signup bonuses. Their presence still changes team-member counts. Realistic VIP, purchase, and bonus examples may be shown as display-only previews, never as financial ledger records. A WhatsApp preview may open a prefilled message without a recipient. Home-page activity snippets must remain display-only and financially inert.

**Why:** The user chose persistent test accounts over a temporary preview, then requested realistic numbers and a visible WhatsApp action. These accounts share the actual referral database; simulated financial activity or a link addressed to an invented number would mislead users or potentially contact unrelated people. The user clarified that removing the standalone banner disclaimer did not mean removing the rotating financial examples themselves.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction whenever referral displays, earnings calculations, activity announcements, contact actions, or cleanup of demo members change. Keep genuine financial totals separate from demonstration data, and never treat these records as genuine paying referrals.

On the homepage, preserve the horizontal notice ticker as decorative display-only content. If a vertical activity feed is requested, render each entry as its own framed block rather than as a table or one shared panel; keep all entries disconnected from actual balances or payout events. Do not add unwanted visible “Exemple”, “Fictif” or “Démo” wording. This does not automatically authorize hiding or deleting separate demo referrals on the Team pages.
