---
name: VIP levels
description: Account VIP badge defaults and how paid offer purchases determine its level.
---

Newly registered users start at VIP 0. The account VIP level is the highest ranked paid offer they have purchased; the first ranked offer maps to VIP 1, the second to VIP 2, and so on. Free offers do not count.

Product display names may be descriptive and must not determine the level. Keep the ranking separate from the display label, using the product catalog order.

The initial CATL catalog enables offers 1–6 for new sales and keeps offers 7–9 inactive without deleting them. Later administrator changes must remain in effect after restart.

**Why:** The user chose descriptive battery-related names instead of the visible VIP-number offer labels, while preserving the account’s VIP ranking.

**Why:** The user requested activating the first six offers and closing the rest.

**How to apply:** Derive the badge from purchase history and offer order. Do not assign a paid VIP level at signup, count a free offer, or parse the display name to infer the level. Apply initial availability only once; do not reset administrator choices on later starts.
